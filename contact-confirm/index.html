You were added as an emergency contact

Someone you know added you as their emergency contact in Eiger, the mountaineering app. Their name is in the email that brought you here.

Eiger does not call rescue services. If an email says they have not checked in, try to reach them, and if you cannot, contact the local emergency number yourself.
